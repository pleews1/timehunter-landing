What exactly is the AI Voice Assistant layer?
It is an optional, high-fidelity conversational agent connected to local phone lines. It picks up incoming queries, fields customer questions from your company playbook, qualifies caller intent, and sets appointments directly into available slots.

Yes, absolutely. We can integrate payment rails (like Square or Stripe) natively into your custom booking flow so clients must commit via authorization hold or fixed deposit to confirm a premium spot.

The engine relies strictly on your predefined availability rule blocks. Clients and AI assistants can only execute a booking on hours you explicitly open for business.
