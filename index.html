FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does deployment take?

Most standard builds are live within 3–7 business days. Complex multi-location or full AI Voice Agent setups can take up to 14 days. We handle everything technical.

Do I need to change my existing calendar or software?

No. We integrate directly with your existing Google Calendar, Outlook 365, or Apple Calendar via real-time two-way sync. Your current schedule and internal blocks are fully respected.

What does the AI Voice Assistant actually do?

It answers calls in your business name, answers FAQs using your knowledge base, qualifies leads, and books appointments directly into your live calendar — even after hours.

Can we require deposits?

Yes. We integrate Square or Stripe directly into the booking flow so clients must commit with a deposit or card hold. You fully control the amount and refund rules.

Are there any hidden recurring fees from TimeHunter?

None from us. Our pricing is one-time. You only pay the normal fees from Square, Stripe, Twilio, or your calendar provider. We never mark up these costs.
