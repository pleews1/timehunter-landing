The Problem

The Hidden Revenue Leak in Service Businesses

Manual workflows, fragmented calendars, and unreturned calls are structural growth bottlenecks costing thousands in lost client lifetime value — every single week.

📵

The Inbound Silence

62% of inbound calls to small-to-medium businesses go completely unanswered. If they reach voicemail, they usually hang up and call a competitor within 60 seconds.

📧

The Endless Back-and-Forth

Hours wasted emailing or texting to find a meeting time that works. Every back-and-forth message is a conversion opportunity slipping away, eroding your professional image.

🪑

The No-Show Cost

Empty appointment slots represent lost margin that can never be recovered. Without dynamic reminders, industry no-show rates hover near 20% — that's 1 in 5 sessions written off.

Closed After Hours

Your best leads are browsing at 10pm. Without 24/7 booking capability, those clients book a competitor who is open online — you wake up to missed revenue, not confirmations.

💸

Unpaid Ghost Clients

Bookings with no deposit commitment attract unqualified leads. Window shoppers clog your calendar, blocking paying clients and causing last-minute cancellations with zero accountability.

📋

Admin Overload

Time spent on manual scheduling, follow-up emails, and rescheduling is time stolen from billable work. Automation eliminates this overhead entirely, reclaiming 8–15 hours weekly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our implementation process and what to expect.

How long does deployment take?
Most standard builds are completely live within 3–7 business days. Complex multi-location or enterprise setups with full AI Voice Agent integration may take up to 14 days. We handle every step — from calendar configuration to payment gateway testing — so you don't need to manage anything technical.
Do I need to change my existing calendar or software?
No. We integrate directly with your existing Google Calendar, Outlook 365, or Apple Calendar in real time via two-way sync. Your current schedule, personal blocks, and internal events are fully read and respected — nothing changes on your end. If you use a CRM, we can typically integrate that too.
What exactly does the AI Voice Assistant do?
Your AI Voice Agent answers inbound calls in your business name using a natural, conversational voice. It answers questions drawn from your business knowledge base (services, pricing, location, policies), qualifies callers based on your criteria, and books or reschedules appointments directly into your live calendar. It can also handle after-hours calls, reducing missed opportunities to zero.
Can we require deposits to filter unqualified leads?
Absolutely. We integrate payment gateways (Square or Stripe) directly into your booking flow so clients must commit via a credit card hold or fixed deposit to confirm a slot. You control the deposit amount, refund policies, and cancellation penalty rules. This is one of the most powerful tools for reducing no-shows and ghost bookings.
What booking channels will my clients be able to use?
Your system can accept bookings via your website (embedded widget or standalone booking page), mobile, direct SMS or email links, Google Business profile integration, and Instagram or Facebook social profiles — plus phone via the AI Voice Agent. We configure whichever channels make sense for your business model and target audience.
Can clients reschedule or cancel themselves?
Yes. Every confirmation and reminder message includes a smart rescheduling link that allows clients to move their own appointment within the rules you set — for instance, no changes within 24 hours of the booking. This eliminates a significant volume of inbound support calls and keeps your team free to focus on billable work.
What happens if a client tries to book outside my business hours?
Your system only exposes time slots you explicitly mark as available. Clients — and the AI Agent — can only book within your defined availability rules. You control buffer time between appointments, days off, lunch blocks, and how far in advance clients can schedule, down to the hour.
Are there any hidden recurring fees from TimeHunter AI?
None from us. Our pricing is a one-off technical delivery and optimisation fee. You pay only the standard rates from the underlying platforms you use — such as Square or Stripe merchant processing fees, or Twilio call rates for the Voice Agent. We don't mark up these costs or charge ongoing retainers.
Can the system handle multiple staff and multiple locations?
Yes. We build multi-staff routing logic so bookings are allocated based on each team member's real-time availability, specialty, or on a round-robin basis. Multi-location setups are fully supported — clients can select a specific location, and availability is managed independently per venue, all within a single admin dashboard.
